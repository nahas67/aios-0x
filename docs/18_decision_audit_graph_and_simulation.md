## 1. System Overview & Purpose

This specification establishes two advanced architectural engines within AIOS:

1. **Decision Audit Graph Engine**: Implements line-of-sight provenance tracking that connects executed trades all the way back to raw source evidence.
2. **Digital Twin & Simulation Layer**: Multi-tier simulation framework providing historical replay, synthetic market generation, agent-vs-agent competition, and black swan stress testing.

---

## 2. Decision Audit Graph Specification

The Decision Audit Graph provides complete backwards and forwards lineage for every action taken by AIOS.

```
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                        DECISION AUDIT GRAPH LINEAGE                               |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
  [Raw Market & News Data]    (Community 1 Payload)
           |
           v
  [Candidate Hypothesis]      (Community 2 Research Thesis + Debate Transcript)
           |
           v
  [Verification Report]       (Community 3 Fact-Check & Confidence Score)
           |
           v
  [Strategy Specification]    (Community 4 Entry/Exit & Position Parameters)
           |
           v
  [Portfolio Allocation Plan] (Community 9 Correlation & Drawdown Check)
           |
           v
  [Risk Firewall Evaluation]  (Non-LLM Circuit Breaker Approval)
           |
           v
  [Trade Execution Receipt]   (Community 5 Exchange Fill Details)
           |
           v
  [Observation Report]        (Community 6 PnL & Post-Mortem Lessons)
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

### 2.1 Graph Node & Edge Schema
Audit graph records are indexed in PostgreSQL and mirrored in Community 7 (Memory Architecture):

```python
class AuditGraphNode(BaseModel):
    node_id: str = Field(default_factory=generate_uuid)
    node_type: Literal[
        "RAW_DATA",
        "HYPOTHESIS",
        "VERIFICATION",
        "STRATEGY",
        "ALLOCATION",
        "RISK_CHECK",
        "EXECUTION",
        "OBSERVATION",
    ] = Field(...)
    timestamp: datetime = Field(default_factory=generate_utc_now)
    payload_id: str = Field(..., description="ID of the underlying payload contract")
    agent_id: str = Field(..., description="Agent or service responsible for this node")
    parent_node_ids: list[str] = Field(
        default_factory=list, description="IDs of ancestor nodes leading to this decision"
    )
    child_node_ids: list[str] = Field(
        default_factory=list, description="IDs of descendant nodes resulting from this decision"
    )
    provenance_metadata: dict[str, Any] = Field(default_factory=dict)
```

### 2.2 Provenance Query API
- `get_decision_provenance(execution_id)` — Returns full DAG chain from `execution_id` backwards to original raw market feeds and news articles.
- `audit_hypothesis_impact(hypothesis_id)` — Returns all strategies, executions, PnL, and observation reports generated by a specific hypothesis.

## 3. Digital Twin & Simulation Layer Specification

The Digital Twin Environment provides a high-fidelity market sandbox that simulates exchange matching engines, market participant behavior, and extreme tail-risk events.

```
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                      DIGITAL TWIN & SIMULATION SUITE                              |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
  TIER 1: Historical Replay         Tick-by-tick playback of real historical crashes.
  TIER 2: Synthetic Market Generator Stochastic & GAN-based artificial price paths.
  TIER 3: Agent vs. Agent Arena     Adversarial paper trading competition.
  TIER 4: Black Swan Stress Test    Extreme liquidity crunches, spread spikes, & gaps.
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

### 3.1 Tier 1: Historical Replay Engine
- Replays stored tick/bar data from TimescaleDB at configurable speeds ($1\times$ to $100\times$).
- Preserves historical order book depth and order execution timestamps.

### 3.2 Tier 2: Synthetic Market Generator
- Uses Geometric Brownian Motion (GBM), GARCH volatility modeling, and synthetic GAN generators to create infinite non-repeating price paths.
- Evaluates strategy performance under non-stationary regimes that have not yet occurred in history.

### 3.3 Tier 3: Agent vs. Agent Competition Arena
- Simulates an internal paper trading order book where research and strategy agents compete against each other.
- Evaluates agent adaptability when competing against adversarial AI strategies rather than static retail flow.

### 3.4 Tier 4: Black Swan Stress Test Engine
- Simulates extreme structural market failures:
  - Exchange WebSocket disconnection (10s–300s outage).
  - Bid-ask spread widening by $2000\%$.
  - Instantaneous $20\%$ price gap down across all correlated assets.
- Verifies that `RiskFirewall` emergency kill-switches trigger cleanly without capital leakage.
